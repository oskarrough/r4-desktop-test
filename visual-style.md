# Visual style

A small instrument you keep close, not an app that asks for attention: calm, precise, quietly warm, a little nostalgic.
Materials: bead-blasted aluminium, soft-touch plastic, fine woven steel mesh, matte glass over a dark screen. Nothing glossy.
Textures: perforated grilles, knurled metal, faint grain in a dusk gradient. Surfaces feel touched, not rendered.
Tones: warm near-black (#121211), off-white type (#EDEDEB), stone grey for what's secondary (#8C8C88), hairlines that barely separate (#2A2A28).
Colour only where something is alive: a red record light, an orange dot, a glow from lilac to amber. One at a time, and small.
Shapes: circles and soft rectangles, keys that look pressable, pills that float. Corners are soft and edges exact.
Type is plain and sure of itself, with figures of even width. Labels are small, titles firm but never loud.
One grid, generous margins, everything lined up to the pixel. Empty space is part of the design, not leftover.
Motion is short and eased, like a well-damped fader: things glide to where they belong and settle. The feeling is a radio you trust, made by someone who cared.
